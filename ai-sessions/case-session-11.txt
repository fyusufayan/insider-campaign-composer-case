  2. Objective                                                                  
  Implement a deterministic, reusable content-domain and validation layer for   
  personalized campaign messages.                                               
  It must provide a safe boundary between untrusted AI-generated text and       
  content that can eventually be persisted, approved and delivered.             
  The layer should support the established SMS/email content model and be       
  usable by:                                                                    
                                                                                
  * The future AI content generator.                                            
  * A future manual content editor.                                             
  * The future approval preparation step.                                       
  * The future delivery worker.                                                 
                                                                                
  Do not couple content validation to the Anthropic SDK.                        
  3. Content domain                                                             
  Implement the content types and invariants defined by the architecture.       
  Cover:                                                                        
                                                                                
  * SMS message body.                                                           
  * Email subject.                                                              
  * Email body.                                                                 
  * Channel-specific content.                                                   
  * Personalization placeholders.                                               
  * Content validation results.                                                 
  * Content version metadata, where appropriate for this checkpoint.            
                                                                                
  Keep content definitions typed and explicit.                                  
  Do not use generic maps or unvalidated `json.RawMessage` as the trusted       
  internal content model.                                                       
  Use the existing database schema as the source of truth for persisted         
  representations.                                                              
  4. Personalization placeholders                                               
  Implement strict recognition and validation of the approved personalization   
  syntax.                                                                       
  Read the actual placeholder catalog and allowed fields from the architecture. 
  Do not assume arbitrary template variables are safe.                          
  Requirements:                                                                 
                                                                                
  * Reject unknown or misspelled placeholders.                                  
  * Reject unsupported template expressions, nested expressions and arbitrary   
  function calls.                                                               
  * Reject malformed or unbalanced template delimiters.                         
  * Avoid accidentally interpreting unknown placeholders as ordinary literal    
  text.                                                                         
  * Define how placeholders are rendered safely with customer-specific values   
  at delivery time, following existing decisions.                               
  * Do not expose customer contact data or personalized values to the LLM.      
                                                                                
  The validator should work on template content, not on customer-specific       
  rendered content.                                                             
  If the architecture specifies fallback behavior for missing profile fields,   
  implement only the deterministic rules that belong in this layer.             
  5. SMS validation                                                             
  Enforce the architecture's SMS limits exactly.                                
  Include:                                                                      
                                                                                
  * Unicode-aware length handling.                                              
  * The correct treatment of personalization placeholders when calculating      
  message size.                                                                 
  * Any required SMS segment or character limits.                               
  * Rejection of empty/whitespace-only messages.                                
  * Rejection of unsupported control characters.                                
  * No silent truncation.                                                       
                                                                                
  Do not invent arbitrary SMS limits. Use the documented limits.                
  6. Email validation                                                           
  Enforce the documented constraints for:                                       
                                                                                
  * Subject length.                                                             
  * Body length.                                                                
  * Allowed formatting, if any.                                                 
  * Safe representation of user-facing content.                                 
  * Personalization placeholders.                                               
  * Empty required fields.                                                      
  * Unsupported control characters or dangerous markup.                         
                                                                                
  Do not confuse email template validation with HTML escaping at                
  delivery/render time.                                                         
  If the architecture supports plain-text email only at this stage, do not      
  introduce a rich HTML editor or HTML sanitizer.                               
  7. Validation errors                                                          
  Provide structured, field-level errors consistent with the existing           
  campaign/segment conventions.                                                 
  For example, distinguish:                                                     
                                                                                
  * Missing SMS body.                                                           
  * SMS too long.                                                               
  * Invalid or unknown placeholder.                                             
  * Missing email subject.                                                      
  * Email subject too long.                                                     
  * Invalid email body.                                                         
                                                                                
  The error messages must be useful for both AI repair prompts and frontend     
  validation without echoing sensitive customer data.                           
  Keep the error shape stable and deterministic.                                
  8. Versioning and immutability boundaries                                     
  Review the existing content-version and approval schema.                      
  Define and implement the domain rules that can safely be established in CP11: 
                                                                                
  * What constitutes a valid content version.                                   
  * Which fields belong to one version.                                         
  * How channel content relates to selected campaign channels.                  
  * Which changes should create a new version.                                  
  * How content versions can eventually be frozen by approval.                  
                                                                                
  Avoid writing incomplete approval or persistence workflows.                   
  Do not alter migration schemas unless the existing design has a demonstrated  
  inconsistency that blocks this checkpoint.                                    
  Prefer pure domain functions and types in CP11. Database writes belong in the 
  next checkpoint unless explicitly required by the established roadmap.        
  9. Security properties                                                        
  Ensure that:                                                                  
                                                                                
  * AI-produced text is treated as untrusted.                                   
  * Unapproved placeholder syntax cannot reach delivery.                        
  * LLM output cannot execute commands or templates.                            
  * Customer personal information never enters AI prompts.                      
  * No raw customer records are included in content-domain errors or logs.      
  * Templates cannot silently widen the intended audience.                      
  * Invalid content is rejected, not truncated or silently repaired.            
                                                                                
  Do not add external template engines or evaluate arbitrary expressions.       
  10. Tests

  6. Unicode and Turkish text.                                                  
  7. SMS boundary lengths.

  9. Whitespace and control characters.                                         
  10. Multiple placeholders and repeated placeholders.                          
  11. Missing personalization values and documented fallback behavior.          
  12. Invalid content for a selected channel.                                   
  13. Valid content for multiple channels.

  4–5. Limits and how characters are counted

  - Limits: SMS 160, subject 80, body 5,000. They apply to the worst-case count 
    of Unicode characters (code points) after NFC normalization and 
    personalization. Each placeholder counts as the larger of its variable's
    maximum and its fallback's length.
  - Normalization: text is normalized to NFC and trimmed. Anything over the
    limit is rejected, never truncated.
  - Line breaks: allowed in SMS and email body, not in the subject. Every other
    control character is rejected, including tab, \r, bidirectional-override
    characters and the BOM.
  - Empty text: text that is blank or contains only invisible characters is
    treated as empty.
  - Markup: markup such as <p> is rejected because content is plain text only.
  - Rendering:
    - A missing value uses its fallback. With no fallback, rendering fails; it
      never produces something like "Hi ,".
    - An over-length or control-character value fails rendering. It is not
      truncated and does not switch to the fallback.
    - The rendered length is checked again at the end.
    - The fixed email footer is added outside the 5,000-character body budget.

  6. Versioning and approval

  - When a new version is created:
    - An AI generation always creates a new version.
    - An edit whose normalized text equals the current version creates nothing.
      This prevents an unchanged save from revoking an approval.
    - Any other change creates version n+1.
  - Provenance: AI versions require a model and a prompt version; edited
    versions must have neither.
  - Channels:
    - Every field that is provided gets validated, whether or not its channel is
      selected.
    - Selected channels must have their fields filled in.
    - Subject and body must be provided together.
  - Approval: approval will bind the immutable version id. Approval preparation
    should call Parse(stored, selectedChannels) again.
